I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the [Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing](https://simons.berkeley.edu/homepage), hosted by [Peter Bartlett](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~bartlett/) and [Bin Yu](https://binyu.stat.berkeley.edu/).  I earned my PhD at the University of Texas at Austin under the wonderful supervision of [Rachel Ward](https://oden.utexas.edu/people/directory/rachel--ward/).  Before that I was at the University of Chicago.

My [research](https://rhshi.github.io/research/) lies broadly in the foundations of data science.
